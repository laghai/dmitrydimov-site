Privacy Policy for Dmitry Dimov Consulting test apps

Effective October 2, 2026. Applies to pre-release test apps distributed by Dmitry Dimov Consulting through Apple TestFlight, including PGU Tune.

What the app collects

The app uses the phone's camera to photograph a test pattern shown on a display, in order to measure and correct how that display shows images. It collects only those photographs, the measurements derived from them, and basic technical information about the device and the session (device model, operating system version, screen size, timestamps).

How it is used

This information is used only to calibrate the display under test and to diagnose and improve the app. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties except the cloud provider that hosts our private servers.

Where it is stored

Depending on the mode, photographs stay on the phone or are uploaded to a private, access-controlled server operated by Dmitry Dimov Consulting. Test data is deleted when it is no longer needed for the test.

Your choices

Camera access is requested by the app and can be revoked at any time in the phone's Settings. To ask for your test data to be deleted, contact us.
